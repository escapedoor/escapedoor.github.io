READ, AND DRAWN · FOR FIFTY YEARS

What you now know
  1. Small words of place are exact: ALONG follows, ACROSS crosses, THROUGH goes in and out, OVER goes up and down, PAST goes by, BETWEEN has a thing on each side, AS FAR AS stops.
  2. TO THE NORTH OF a place is outside it; IN THE NORTH OF a place is inside it.
  3. A BEARING is counted in degrees, clockwise from north: east is 90°, and 100° is a little south of east.
  4. A river’s banks are named LOOKING DOWNSTREAM, from the source towards the mouth. On a north-up map, a river that flows south has its left bank on the right.
  5. Every map has a SCALE and a KEY. Make the scales and the north points agree before you lay one map on another.
  6. A right to cross, to graze or to fish is not a right to own. MAY grants, SHALL binds, and a later clause can take back part of an earlier one.
  7. Words move. LET once meant hinder, PREVENT go before, MEAT food, NICE exact. An old text means what its words meant then: prove it from papers of the same age.
