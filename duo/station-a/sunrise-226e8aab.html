What you now know
  • An orbit four hundred kilometres up takes about ninety-two minutes: here about fifty-six minutes of day and thirty-six of night, so about sixteen sunrises a day.
  • A checklist works only in the right order: a hatch that opens inwards is held shut by the air until the gauge reads zero.
  • On a spacewalk you are always clipped on: clip the new tether before you unclip the old one, and tether every tool.
  • On the radio, numbers go digit by digit and every instruction is read back: one five and five zero were different handrails.
  • Left, right, top and bottom depend on where you are: upside down, a drawing’s top left is your bottom right.
  • A torque stripe is a line of paint that breaks if a bolt turns.
  • A manual can be out of date. When the hardware and the page disagree, believe the hardware, and find out why.
